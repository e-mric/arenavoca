## Recording plan

The source clip is 36 seconds. Record multiple clearly cut takes; do not stretch
it or imply that it is a continuous two-minute match. In the uninterrupted
playback take, start opening the pass at eight seconds and reveal English before
the clip ends. Capture recall and persistence in separate takes after playback.
If more time is needed, explicitly pause or restart before recording that take.

Record 1920×1080, retain a visible remote focus indicator and hold readable states
for at least two seconds. Prefer short cuts over cursor demonstrations. Do not
use generated concept screens as execution evidence. Keep the final export below
three minutes; the timecodes above are editing targets, not measured voice timing.

The mobile comic experience can get a separate video. This cut concentrates on
the working Vega submission and does not imply that comics or phone sync run on TV.

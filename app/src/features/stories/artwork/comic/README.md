# Prepared manhwa illustrations

Generated: 2026-10-21 with Codex built-in image generation. Full authoring prompts
are in [prompts.json](prompts.json). Three original 1536 × 1024 PNGs are retained:

- [Alba run](../res/drawable-nodpi/comic_alba_run.png)
- [Alba cutback](../res/drawable-nodpi/comic_alba_cutback.png)
- [Coutinho finish](../res/drawable-nodpi/comic_alba_goal.png)

Inputs were authored descriptions and the ArenaVoca palette, without broadcast
frames or third-party artwork. The panels have no baked-in captions, logos or
sponsors. They are explanatory illustrations, not exact reconstructions of player
locations, anatomy, kits or ball trajectories. The cutback arrow points backward
from the byline toward the arriving teammate. AI generation and visual inspection
do not establish factual accuracy, brand clearance or language review.

Native UI supplies source-backed Spanish and English separately. Original
recap/story artwork remains unchanged. The [prototype guide](https://github.com/e-mric/arenavoca/blob/full-project/app/src/features/stories/COMIC.md) (on `full-project`)
describes use, interaction and verification boundaries.

The mobile-only comic extension (run, cross, volley) and its prompts are on the
`full-project` branch.
